# Returns Policy

This document describes how customers may return purchased items for a
refund or exchange, the conditions under which a return is accepted, and the
steps a support agent should follow when processing a request. It applies to
all storefronts operated by Acme and supersedes any earlier version of this
policy.

## Refund window

### Standard purchases

Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a
full refund to the original payment method. The item must be unused, in its
original packaging, and accompanied by the order confirmation or receipt.
Refunds are issued once the returned item has been received and inspected by
the warehouse team.

### Promotional purchases

Items bought during a promotional sale carry a shortened 14 day return
window from the delivery date. Promotional items are refunded at the price
actually paid, not the original list price, and store credit is offered as
an alternative to a cash refund when the customer prefers to keep the
promotional discount applied to a future order.

## Exceptions

Not every item purchased from Acme is eligible for return, and this section
lists the categories that are excluded along with the reasoning behind each
exclusion so that support agents can explain a denial consistently rather
than improvising a justification on the spot.

Perishable goods, including refrigerated and frozen items, cannot be
returned once they leave the warehouse because Acme has no way to verify
that proper cold-chain handling was maintained in transit or in the
customer's possession. The same reasoning applies to any item explicitly
marked as final sale at the time of purchase; those listings carry a lower
price precisely because the return path has been removed from the cost
structure, and honoring a return after the fact would undermine the pricing
model for every other customer who paid full price expecting the standard
return window.

Custom or personalized items, such as engraved jewelry, monogrammed apparel,
or made-to-order furniture, are excluded because they were manufactured
specifically for one customer and cannot be resold. Gift cards and other
stored-value instruments are non-returnable for the same reason a cash
refund would be for currency: there is no product to inspect or restock.

Items showing evidence of use beyond simple inspection are treated as
damaged goods rather than as a standard return. A damaged-goods claim
requires photographic evidence submitted within 7 days of delivery, a
description of the damage, and, where applicable, the original packaging
showing how the item was shipped. Claims submitted after the 7 day window
are evaluated case by case and are frequently denied, since the delay makes
it difficult to distinguish shipping damage from damage that occurred during
customer use. Support agents should never promise a damaged-goods refund
before the photographic evidence has been reviewed by the returns team,
because a premature promise creates a commitment the company may not be
able to honor once the evidence arrives.

Proof of purchase is required for every return regardless of category. An
order confirmation email, a packing slip, or a receipt showing the Acme
order number all satisfy this requirement. Returns without any proof of
purchase may still be accepted at the discretion of a supervisor, but only
as store credit at the current listed price, never as a cash refund, since
Acme cannot otherwise confirm the amount the customer actually paid or
whether the item was purchased through an authorized channel at all.

Health and personal care items, including cosmetics, skincare, and any
product that has broken a hygiene seal, are excluded once the seal has been
broken, for the same public-health reasoning that governs pharmacy returns
generally: a product that has left its sealed packaging cannot be verified
as safe to resell to another customer.

## How to start a return

Customers can begin a return from the order history page by selecting the
item and choosing a reason from the provided list. The system generates a
prepaid return label for eligible items, and the refund is issued after the
warehouse confirms receipt and passes inspection.
