# Billing FAQ

This document answers the billing questions support agents are asked most
often, covering how invoices are generated, which payment methods are
accepted, and how a customer disputes a charge they believe is incorrect.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and reflect
usage from the previous cycle plus any recurring subscription fee. An
invoice is considered final once it has been issued; corrections to a
finalized invoice are handled as a credit or a charge on the following
invoice rather than by reissuing the original document. Customers can
download a PDF copy of any invoice from the billing portal at any time.

## Payment methods

Globex accepts payment by credit card, ACH bank transfer, and, for accounts
above a negotiated spending threshold, invoiced net-30 terms. Credit card
payments are charged automatically on the invoice date. ACH and net-30
customers must remit payment manually, and an account more than 15 days
past due on a net-30 invoice is moved to automatic credit-card billing for
subsequent cycles unless the customer's account manager approves an
exception.

## Disputes

A customer who believes an invoice is incorrect should open a dispute
through the billing portal within 60 days of the invoice date; disputes
opened after that window are reviewed only at the discretion of the billing
team. Opening a dispute does not pause the payment due date automatically,
so a customer paying by net-30 terms should still remit payment for the
undisputed portion of the invoice while the disputed line items are under
review. Once a dispute is resolved, any resulting credit or refund is
applied to the account within one billing cycle.
